# Sections, Solos and Freestyle

*New in 3.0.*

Every song now knows its **sections**: intro, verses, choruses, solos, breaks and outro. They're worked out from the charts (how busy each part is, whether anyone sings, the harmony and melody that come back), so older songs get them too, with no re-import.

## Solos

When a guitar or keys solo starts, the soloing part sees **GUITAR SOLO!** and a counter under the song title (hits / notes and %). At the end you get a callout like **SOLO 87%** or **PERFECT SOLO!**, and the results screen shows how each solo went.

## Drum fills

When a drummer's overdrive is ready, the first notes of each new section **glow**. Hit them and overdrive launches, like ending a fill on the crash. The overdrive button still works too.

## Big rock ending

When the band rings out after the last note, the stage calls a **BIG ROCK ENDING**. Every hit in the ring-out fires the pyro, and the results show how many you got.

## Practice from a section

*Practice → Section* steps through the song's sections: start right at *Chorus 2* or the *Guitar solo*. The start slider still works for any bar.

## Freestyle solos

*Settings → Gameplay → Freestyle solos (off by default).*

In a guitar or keys solo the notes drop away and **you improvise**:

- **The sound:** your frets play the song's own instrument. Clean single notes are taken from its stem and pitched to a five-note (pentatonic) scale in the song's key, centred on where the solo sits.
- **Strum mode:** strum the held frets. A fret pressed while a note rings plays like a hammer-on.
- **Tap mode:** press a fret to play it.
- **Whammy:** bends what's ringing.
- **The original solo stops.** Yours replaces it.

The freestyle is graded on **groove** (notes on the 16th-note grid), **flow** (a playable number of notes per beat) and **melody** (several frets, few repeats, a line that moves).

## Bonuses and the leaderboards

- Solos, fills and endings pay **cash and XP**, never score, so leaderboards stay comparable.
- Freestyle runs drop the solo's notes, so they're kept off the world leaderboard and your personal bests.
- Freestyle isn't available in practice or online.
