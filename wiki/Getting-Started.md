# Getting Started

## Your first song

1. Start STEMSTAGE and pick or create a **profile** (name, colour, optional PIN). *Guest* plays without saving.
2. The built-in demo song **Neon Overdrive** is ready straight away. Open **Play → Solo**, pick it, choose an instrument and difficulty, and press **Play**.
3. To add your own music, open **Import**:
   - **Audio files:** drop MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG or M4A files, or a whole folder.
   - **YouTube:** search, or paste a video or playlist link (uses yt-dlp).

   Importing splits the song into stems (with the AI splitter: 1–3 minutes on an NVIDIA GPU), transcribes the notes and builds four difficulties. Title, artist, album and cover art are looked up automatically (MusicBrainz, iTunes).

## Instruments

| Part | Lanes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Guitar / Bass | 5 frets | Strum mode (press + strum) or tap mode. Hammer-ons/pull-offs in strum mode |
| Drums | 4 pads + kick | Cymbal gems for the pro-drums look |
| Keys | 5 lanes | Or play on a MIDI keyboard ([Real Instruments](Real-Instruments)) |
| Vocals | Pitch track | Sing into a mic ([Singing and Lyrics](Singing-and-Lyrics)) or play with buttons |

**Overdrive:** hit every note of a glowing phrase to fill the meter, then tilt the guitar, press the overdrive button, or hit both cymbals on drums. It doubles your multiplier and revives failed bandmates.

## Song options

Press **△ / Y / Tab** on a song for: Practice (slow down, start anywhere), Replays & ghosts, Add to setlist, **World charts** (play the ranked chart or another uploaded one, vote, or share yours), Edit chart, **Get lyrics (AI)**, **Check lyrics online**, Re-chart with AI, Edit song info, Export chart pack (Clone Hero / YARG), Delete.

## Pro mode

*Settings → Gameplay → Pro mode* is for when Expert gets easy:
- The timing is tighter: 80 ms to hit a note, 25 ms for a Perfect.
- Overstrums and wrong frets break your streak.
- Assists and no-fail are off.

Pro runs get 25% more XP, and PRO shows in the song title and on your results. Real guitar, bass and keys ([Real Instruments](Real-Instruments)) are the game's pro instrument modes.

## Tour, career and challenges

**Tour** takes you through seven venues, from the Garage to a festival main stage, unlocked by stars. Each gig is played in its own venue. The stage really changes: a brick-walled garage with a handful of friends, a hazy club and dive bar, a theatre with a red curtain, and an arena, stadium and festival under the night sky. Once a venue is unlocked you can play everything there (*Settings → Video → Venue*). The **daily challenge** keeps a day streak.

The **weekly challenge** (Tour → Weekly & season) is one song part for everyone that week:
- The song comes from those with a ranked chart.
- Your best run on its ranked chart that week counts.
- Your place earns season points: 100 for first, down to 10 for taking part.
- A season is six weeks, and the standings are in the game and on the website.
- If you don't have the song, **Find it on YouTube** imports it under the right title, so the boards match.

**Chart library** (main menu) lists every song players have charted, with its ranked parts. Songs you have open in the setlist; for the others, **Get it** searches YouTube.

**Character** (Career → Character) dresses your band member:
- Skin, hair style and colour, top, trousers and instrument finish, or one of eight ready-made looks.
- The part you stand at in the menus.

Your character takes your part's place on stage when you play, and friends see it in online rooms. Your **career** page shows XP, level, 34 achievements, history and your linked [Discord](Discord) account. **Share profile** publishes it as a web page anyone can open (`stemstage.varconstint.com/player/?id=…`): level and rank, achievements, stats, world rank and world records. It's copied to your clipboard, ready to paste in Discord. The page updates by itself after every run while *Settings → World leaderboard* is on.

The **world leaderboard** (Leaderboards → World, or [on the website](https://stemstage.varconstint.com/leaderboard/)) ranks everyone's best runs per song, instrument and difficulty. Names link to profile pages.
