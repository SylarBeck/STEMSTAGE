# Worlds and Bosses

STEMSTAGE 2.0 has five worlds: full 3D stages with their own sky, set, lights and atmosphere. Each has a boss.

| World | Boss | Attacks | Opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abyssal Aquarium | The Leviathan | Ink cloud, bubble wall, riptide | From the start |
| Orbital Nebula | The Void Conductor | Gravity well, blackout, meteor shower | Level 5, or beat the Leviathan |
| Volcanic Forge | The Magma Titan | Heat haze, earthquake, ash storm | Level 9, or beat the Void Conductor |
| Crystal Aurora | The Frost Wyrm | Frostbite, whiteout, shatter | Level 13, or beat the Magma Titan |
| Storm Citadel | The Thunderbird | Lightning strike, hurricane, static storm | Level 17, or beat the Frost Wyrm |

Pick a world per song (Setlist → **Stage**), in Settings → Video → Venue, or as the host of an online room.

## Boss fights

In a world, the boss can show up in the middle of a song (Settings → Gameplay → **World bosses**: random, always or off). A warning sounds, the boss arrives, and a health bar and timer appear.

- **Every note you hit damages it.** Perfect hits do the most, a long streak adds up to +50%, overdrive hits 2.5× harder, and the Boss damage upgrades and pedals add more. Singing: every rated phrase counts.
- **Every miss lets it recover** a little.
- **It attacks your highway** every couple of bars: clouds, bubbles, swaying or warping notes, frost creeping in, a mirrored highway, lightning on a lane. Timing never changes, only what you see. **Settings → Gameplay → Boss hazards → Mild** makes them lighter and never mirrors the highway (Calm visuals does this too).
- **Beat it before the timer runs out** for its bounty (cash and XP, more the first time) and, the first time, its loot: a boss pedal, wardrobe pieces, the next world and a trophy for the stage creator. If time runs out it escapes.

Bosses never change your score, so leaderboards stay fair. Signed-in kills go to the **[boss hall of fame](https://stemstage.varconstint.com/play/#bosses)** (fastest and most kills).

Achievements: **Monster Hunter** (beat a boss), **Untouchable** (beat one without missing during the fight), **Slayer of Worlds** (beat all five).

Online, the room's seed decides when the boss comes, so everyone meets it at the same moment. In **band** matches the whole band shares one health bar.
