STEMSTAGE
any song · any instrument · split by AI

The rhythm game that plays any song

Drop in a track and an AI splits it into stems. Every part becomes a playable chart: guitar, bass, drums, keys and vocals. Play with a controller, Rock Band gear, a DualSense, or your real guitar, bass and keyboard.

How it works

Your music, charted in minutes

1

Drop in any song

An audio file, a folder, or a YouTube search or playlist. Title, artist and cover art are filled in for you.

2

AI splits it into stems

Demucs separates drums, bass, guitar, piano and vocals on your GPU. basic-pitch transcribes the notes and Whisper hears the lyrics.

3

Play any part

Four difficulties with overdrive phrases for every instrument. Solo, as a four-piece band, or online with friends.

Features

Everything the band needs

Any song, any part

Demucs htdemucs_6s stems, neural note transcription, and an auto-charter that builds Easy to Expert.

Real instruments New

Plug in a real guitar or bass and read scrolling tablature, or play keys on a MIDI keyboard with notes falling onto a piano.

DualSense, done properly

Adaptive triggers that click like frets, haptics, lightbar and player LEDs over USB and Bluetooth, with near-zero input lag.

Sing, with checked lyrics New

Karaoke pitch scoring with AI lyrics, cross-checked against the LRCLIB lyrics database so misheard words get fixed.

Discord New

Link your profile, show what you're playing, and let friends press Join to hop into your online room.

Every controller

Xbox, DualShock, Switch Pro, Joy-Con, Rock Band and Guitar Hero guitars and drums, MIDI kits, keyboard. Each gets its own profile.

Band & online

Up to four local players, or online rooms with an invite code and no port forwarding. Songs are sent to your friends for you.

Tour & career

Seven venues from the garage to a festival main stage, daily challenges, XP, 34 achievements and a world leaderboard.

Ghosts & replays

Every run is recorded. Watch it back, or race your best as a ghost.

Chart editor

Fix the AI's charts with a controller, keyboard or mouse, with live playback and undo.

Stream mode

Twitch chat votes for the next song and requests tracks, and an OBS overlay shows it all.

Accessible

Colour-blind palettes, calm visuals, lane assist for one-handed play, auto sustain and a bigger HUD.

Real instrument mode

Learn it on the real thing

Guitar, bass and keys can be played on the real instrument. STEMSTAGE listens to your audio interface or MIDI keyboard and scores every note.

  • Tablature for guitar and bass: string and fret for every note, fingered the way a player would, with chords, sustains and bar lines.
  • Keyboard for keys, with notes falling onto the keys you need to press.
  • Shows what you're playing live: green when it's right, gold when it isn't.
  • Octave-free scoring, and input latency learned while you play.
Play together

Join a friend's room

Got an invite code?

Type the words your friend sent you, like POTTER-SIMON-FLORAL-EDGES. STEMSTAGE opens straight into their room, or you can download it first.

Hosting?

In the game open Online → Host online, then press Invite friends to copy a link. Paste it in Discord: anyone can click it, even friends who don't have STEMSTAGE yet.

One-click install

The installer sets up the game, the DualSense controller bridge and (if you want it) the AI splitter. Nothing else to install.

The AI splitter

A 3–5 GB download during setup (or later from Settings). An NVIDIA GPU makes splitting fast; without one, a built-in splitter is used.
