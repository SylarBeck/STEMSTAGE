# Runs

*New in 3.0. Main menu → Runs.*

A run is a roguelike night built from **your own songs**. Pick an instrument and a difficulty, then climb a map of eight floors to the **headliner**. Every run has a different map, and the **daily run** is the same map for everyone that day (your songs fill it).

## The map

Each floor has three spots, and paths lead up to one or two spots above. Pick where to go next:

| Spot | |
|---|---|
| 🎵 **Gig** | Play a song. Three stars or more pays run cash and lets you pick a perk |
| 🔥 **Encore** | A harder song (one difficulty up) with a **curse** on it. Pays double |
| 🏪 **Merch stand** | Spend run cash on perks ($60) or a heart ($50) |
| 🛋️ **Green room** | Get a heart back |
| ❓ **Backstage pass** | Something happens: a free perk, some cash, or a shady deal |
| 👑 **Headliner** | The hardest song of the night, on the floor above the green room and merch stands. Three stars wins the run |

Songs get harder the higher you climb, and a run doesn't repeat a song while there are others to play.

## Hearts

You start with **three hearts**. A song under three stars, or a fail, costs one. Lose them all and the run is over. Losing to the headliner costs a heart and you can try again.

## Perks

Perks last for the rest of the run and stack. They're gear for one run only:

- **Lucky Pick:** a streak shield, so one miss keeps your streak.
- **Wide Neck:** +10 ms to hit each note.
- **Afterburner:** overdrive lasts 30% longer.
- **Hype Man:** the crowd minds misses 30% less.
- **Fuzz:** overdrive phrases fill 25% more.

Three more join the pool, one for each run you win: **Fast Hands**, **Sustainer** and **Merch Deal**.

## Curses

Encores carry one curse:

- **Mirror:** the highway is flipped.
- **Fog:** the far half of the highway is hidden.
- **Breakneck:** notes come much faster.
- **Sudden death:** no no-fail, and misses cost three times the crowd.
- **Unplugged:** no overdrive.

## Rewards

A finished run pays its run cash into your wallet, plus $150 for a win, and XP for every floor climbed (more for a win). The Runs screen keeps your record: runs won and played, your best floor, and today's daily.

A run is saved on your profile, so you can stop and carry on later. **Abandon run** gives it up.

Runs are their own game, so their songs stay off the world leaderboard.
