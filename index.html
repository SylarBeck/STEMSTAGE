    How to Play

    1 · Import

    Drop any song or search YouTube. AI splits it into drums, bass, guitar, keys and vocals.

    2 · Pick a part

    Every stem is charted automatically in four difficulties.

    3 · Play

    Hit gems as they cross the line. Miss and your part drops out of the mix.

    Overdrive

    Clear silver phrases, then press △ / Enter to double your score.

    Sustains

    Hold long notes and wiggle the stick or whammy bar to bend them.

    Band

    Up to 4 players. Overdrive saves failed bandmates.

    Strum mode

    Guitar controllers: hold frets and strum. White-topped gems are hammer-ons.

    Controllers
