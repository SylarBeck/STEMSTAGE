# Discover

*New in 3.0. Main menu → Discover.*

Discover is your library, thinking. It reads your songs' charts and your play history (nothing is uploaded) and offers three things.

## Smart setlists

Setlists the game builds for the instrument and difficulty you last picked:

| Setlist | |
|---|---|
| **The climb** | About 20 minutes, from your easiest song to your hardest |
| **All killer** | Your busiest songs back to back, slowest tempo first |
| **Dusty records** | Songs you haven't played in a month, or ever |
| **In *key*** | Your songs that share a key (found from the charts' pitches), for a set that flows |
| **Quick set** | Your three shortest songs |
| **Your favourites** | The songs you play most |
| ***Instrument* workout** | Your hardest parts on that instrument |

Pick one to **play it now** (back to back, one combined score, like a setlist marathon) or **save it** to your setlists.

## Because you play…

Songs by artists like the ones you play most. Step through your artists with left/right. **Find** opens a YouTube search to import the song; songs already in your setlist are marked.

Suggestions come from [Deezer](https://www.deezer.com)'s related artists, asked through the game's local server with no account or key. Only the artist's name is sent. The built-in demo band is skipped.

## Your stats

Songs played, hours, days on stage, average accuracy, best streak and full combos, plus your most played song, your main instrument and the hour you usually play. It's all counted from the play history on this PC for your profile.
